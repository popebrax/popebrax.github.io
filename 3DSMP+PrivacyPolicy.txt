1. INTRODUCTION
───────────────
Welcome to 3D Surround Music Player + ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains
how Braxton Otieno ("Developer", "we", "us") handles your information when you
use this App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with
applicable laws and regulations, including those of the People's Republic of
China and relevant international standards.

Please read this policy carefully before using the App. By tapping "Accept" in
the App, you agree to the terms described herein.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
──────────────────────────
2.1 Information You Provide
  • No account registration is required.
  • You may optionally provide feedback or support requests via email.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically
  • Audio file metadata (title, artist, album, duration) stored locally on your
    device — never uploaded to any server.
  • Playback statistics (play counts, recently played) — stored locally only.
  • App preferences and settings — stored locally using Android DataStore.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any
    other personal identifier without your explicit consent.
  • We do not collect, store, or transmit the actual audio files on your device.
  • We do not run advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use tracking technologies, cookies, or fingerprinting.

3. PERMISSIONS AND WHY WE USE THEM
────────────────────────────────────
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — to access audio files stored on
    your device for playback. Required for core functionality.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK — to continue music
    playback in the background and show a media notification. The foreground
    service is started ONLY when you begin playing music. It is not started
    silently or without your initiation. This directly addresses Huawei
    AppGallery rule 2.19 regarding foreground services.
  • RECORD_AUDIO — used solely for the in-app audio visualizer (real-time
    waveform display). Requested only when you activate the visualizer. No audio
    is recorded or stored.
  • WAKE_LOCK — to prevent the CPU from sleeping during active playback.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS — to request audio focus and apply equalizer effects.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to display media playback controls in the notification
    bar (Android 13+).
  • INTERNET — used only to fetch artist images from Last.fm (cover art only).
    No personal data is transmitted.

4. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
────────────────────────────────
  • To provide and improve the music playback experience.
  • To remember your preferences (theme, equalizer settings, etc.).
  • To display playback statistics within the App.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.

5. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY
──────────────────────────────
All data processed by this App is stored exclusively on your device. We do not
operate any servers that receive or store user data. Standard Android OS
security mechanisms protect your locally stored data.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
───────────────────────
This App is not directed at children under the age of 14 (or the applicable age
of consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has used this App and provided
personal data, please contact us so we can address it.

7. YOUR RIGHTS
───────────────
Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:
  • Delete all App data: Android Settings → Apps → 3D Surround Music Player + →
    Clear Data.
  • Revoke any permission: Android Settings → Apps → Permissions.
  • Revoking storage permission will prevent the App from accessing your music
    library.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update
